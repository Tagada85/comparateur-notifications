How much does notification infrastructure cost?

Each vendor bills a different unit: the send, the message per channel, the execution of a workflow. To compare them, this page prices the same usage on the public price list of five vendors, collected on November 5, 2026. Vendors are presented in alphabetical order; the list is not exhaustive, and none pays to be on it.

The scenario

A SaaS product sends notifications to its users, one per recipient. All of them show up in the application’s notification center; half also go out by email. Three monthly volumes, so as not to pick the one that favors a vendor:

  • Launch: 5,000 notifications, 2,500 of which also by email.
  • Growth: 50,000 notifications, 25,000 of which also by email.
  • Volume: 250,000 notifications, 125,000 of which also by email.

What each vendor counts

Courier and Novu bill one unit per recipient: a notification counts as one, whether it goes out in the application only or also by email. Knock, MagicBell, and SuprSend bill each channel: the same notification counts as two. At the Growth volume, the former therefore count 50,000 units, the latter 75,000.

Courier
The send: one template sent to one recipient. A multichannel template counts as a single send, even when it goes out by email, SMS and push; a send to a list of 100 users counts as 100. (source, November 5, 2026)
Knock
The message sent: one message delivered to one user on one channel. A trigger that produces an in-app message and an email counts as two. (source, November 5, 2026)
MagicBell
The delivery, counted per channel: a notification received in the app by 100 users, 50 of whom also get it by email, counts as 150. (source, November 5, 2026)
Novu
The workflow run: one execution of a workflow. Triggering a workflow to a topic of 100 subscribers counts as 100 runs. (source, November 5, 2026)
SuprSend
The notification: one message sent to a user or an object on one channel. A digest that groups several requests counts as one. (source, November 5, 2026)

The calculation in detail

Each amount can be recalculated with the vendor’s price list: plan price, included units, overage price.

Launch: 5,000 notifications, 2,500 of which also by email

  • Courier, Developer: 5,000 sends. Within the 10,000 included: $0.
  • Knock, Free: 7,500 messages. Within the 10,000 included: $0.
  • MagicBell, Startup: 7,500 per-channel deliveries. Within the 50,000 included: $249.
  • Novu, Free: 5,000 workflow runs. Within the 10,000 included: $0.
  • SuprSend, Free: 7,500 notifications. Within the 10,000 included: $0.

Growth: 50,000 notifications, 25,000 of which also by email

  • Courier, Business: 50,000 sends. $0 + (50,000 − 10,000) × $0.005 = $200.
  • Knock, Starter: 75,000 messages. $250 + (75,000 − 50,000) × $0.005 = $375.
  • MagicBell, Startup: 75,000 per-channel deliveries. $249 + (75,000 − 50,000) × $0.0025 = $311.50.
  • Novu, Pro: 50,000 workflow runs. $30 + (50,000 − 30,000) ÷ 1,000 × $1.20 = $54.
  • SuprSend, Essentials: 75,000 notifications. $100 + (75,000 − 50,000) ÷ 1,000 × $2 = $150.

Volume: 250,000 notifications, 125,000 of which also by email

  • Courier, Business: 250,000 sends. $0 + (250,000 − 10,000) × $0.005 = $1,200.
  • Knock, Starter: 375,000 messages. $250 + (375,000 − 50,000) × $0.005 = $1,875.
  • MagicBell, Startup: 375,000 per-channel deliveries. $249 + (375,000 − 50,000) × $0.0025 = $1,061.50.
  • Novu, Team: 250,000 workflow runs. Within the 250,000 included: $250.
  • SuprSend, Essentials: 375,000 notifications. $100 + (375,000 − 50,000) ÷ 1,000 × $2 = $750.

What this calculation does not count

  • Plans on quote and negotiated discounts: only public price lists go into the calculation.
  • The plans’ other limits: team members, environments, history retention, features. The cheapest plan for a volume may not be enough; each tool’s profile gives what was collected.
  • The service that delivers the emails, when the tool goes through yours, and taxes.
  • Currency conversion: each amount is in the currency of the published price list. An exchange rate would make the ranking vary from one day to the next without anything having changed at the vendors.
  • Test sends and failures: the calculation assumes that each notification goes out once.

Prices change: each amount holds for the date it was collected. Has a price changed? Report it.
