Knock or Novu?

Two notification infrastructures for SaaS products, compared on their official documentation, collected from November 4 to 11, 2026. Neither is ranked above the other: the right choice depends on your criteria.

In short

Data hosting
Knock — Knock says it expects to offer data residency in the European Union by the end of 2026. (source, November 5, 2026) Amazon Web Services for hosting and cloud infrastructure, in the United States (subprocessor list updated on June 10, 2026). (source, November 5, 2026)
Novu — EU region in Frankfurt (Germany), chosen when the account is created; other regions (United Kingdom, Singapore, Australia, Japan, South Korea) on Enterprise plans. (source, November 5, 2026) Data residency in the United States or the EU is included from the free plan up. (source, November 5, 2026) The Security page of the documentation gives the regions, not the cloud provider. (source, November 5, 2026)
Deployment
Knock — A managed service: Knock provides the API and the dashboard. (source, November 4, 2026)
Novu — A managed service (Novu Cloud) or a self-hosted community edition, which lacks some Novu Cloud features. (source, November 4, 2026) The code is published under the MIT license, except the “enterprise” code, which is under a commercial license. (source, November 4, 2026)
What is billed
Knock — The message sent: one message delivered to one user on one channel. A trigger that produces an in-app message and an email counts as two. (source, November 5, 2026)
Novu — The workflow run: one execution of a workflow. Triggering a workflow to a topic of 100 subscribers counts as 100 runs. (source, November 5, 2026)

Depending on your need

This site does not name a winner: the right tool depends on what you are looking for. The rows to read first:

Keep data in the European Union
“Data regions”, “Declared cloud provider”: the region and the plan that opens it, then where the subprocessors are and which country’s jurisdiction the vendor falls under. See also Hosting, EU region and subprocessors.
Start without paying
“Free plan”.
A predictable cost as volume grows
“What is billed”, “First paid plan”: count your volume in each vendor’s unit before comparing amounts. See also How much does notification infrastructure cost?
Host the tool yourself
“Deployment model”: this criterion then matters more than the price.
Manage workflows in code and CI
“Workflows as code or via API”, “Deployment model”.
Send to a messaging app
“Channels”.
Drive the tool from an AI assistant
“AI tooling”.

What this comparison does not cover

We have not tested the two products: this page compares what their vendors publish. It does not cover negotiated Enterprise plans, performance or the quality of support. Has a piece of information changed? Report it.
