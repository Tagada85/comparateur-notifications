About & method

How tools are chosen and presented, where the information comes from, and who is speaking to you.

Which tools are listed

  • 25 tools that send messages to a company’s customers: emails, SMS, push notifications, in-app messages, messaging apps. The list is not exhaustive.
  • Each tool is placed in one or more of these categories, based on what its vendor publishes:
    • Email marketing & newsletters: Send campaigns and newsletters to a list of contacts.
    • Customer engagement: Engage customers across several channels, with automated journeys.
    • Push & in-app messaging: Reach the users of a mobile app or a website.
    • Transactional email: Send, from an application, the emails that an action triggers.
    • Messaging APIs: SMS, WhatsApp: Build SMS and WhatsApp into an application, through an API.
    • Notification infrastructure: Orchestrate a product’s notifications, notification center included.
  • No vendor pays to be listed here or to be placed higher, and no link is an affiliate link.

How tools are presented

  • In alphabetical order, except when a table is sorted by a criterion, which is then written above the table.
  • No overall score, no general ranking, no user reviews.
  • One card per tool. Each marker on a card (country, hosting, free plan, use cases, channels) sums up a collected fact, without its conditions: the tool’s profile gives the full fact, with its source and date.
  • A marker only comes from a fact that states it. When a vendor’s page says nothing, the profile says “not collected” or “not specified”, never “no”.

Where the information comes from

  • Every piece of information about a tool comes from its vendor’s official documentation or public pages. It is rephrased, never made up, and carries a link to its source and the date it was collected.
  • On the day of collection, the source page is captured and kept, with a fingerprint that proves the capture has not been modified since.
  • No information older than 6 months is published. After that, it is checked again or removed.
  • Each fact is written in French and in English; both texts state the same figures and the same conditions.

Cookies and scripts

This site sets no cookie. Two pages run a small script, served by the site itself: the name search on the home page and the “Compare” page. It keeps nothing in your browser and calls no other site; without it, the list, the filters and the profiles work. The fonts are served by the site: a page loads nothing from another site.

Report an error

Is a piece of information wrong or out of date, including if you are the vendor concerned? The address to write to will be given here, with the name of this site’s publisher, before publication.

Every fact comes from the vendor’s official page, captured on the day it was collected and reviewed at least every 6 months. Found an error? Report it, and it gets corrected with its source.
