SendGrid

United States · Twilio Inc., San Francisco · Transactional email, Email marketing & newsletters

Official website ↗ Compare with…

SendGrid is Twilio’s email service, with two products sold separately. The Email API is for developers who send transactional email from their application, through an API or over SMTP. Marketing Campaigns is for marketing teams that design, automate and send campaigns to contact lists, without writing code.

Pricing

Free plan

60-day free trial at $0 per month: 100 emails per day, no credit card required. Source: twilio.com/en-us/products/email-api/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

First paid plan

Essentials (Email API): $19.95 per month for 50,000 emails per month, $34.95 for 100,000; overage charges may apply. Source: twilio.com/en-us/products/email-api/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

What is billed

The email sent to one recipient, counted even if it is deferred or sent to spam; Marketing Campaigns is also billed on the number of contacts stored, and its emails are counted separately from Email API emails. Source: twilio.com/en-us/products/marketing-campaigns/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data & hosting

Company behind the service

Twilio Inc., San Francisco (United States); customers domiciled in, among others, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland contract with Twilio Ireland Limited, in Dublin. Source: twilio.com/en-us/legal/tos · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data regions

EU data residency: it requires a subuser designated for the EU, a dedicated EU IP (Pro or Premier plans) and the EU API endpoint; Marketing Campaigns is not available in that environment. Source: twilio.com/docs/sendgrid/data-residency/faq · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Declared cloud provider

Hosting and storage at DataBank and Lumen (North America) and Digital Realty (North America and EU); Amazon Web Services routes and transmits emails (United States and EU) and provides automation and storage for Marketing Campaigns (United States). Source: twilio.com/en-us/legal/sub-processors · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Subprocessor list

Public list shared by all Twilio products, updated in September 2026, with a subscription to changes; for SendGrid, it names Amazon Web Services, Snowflake (Marketing Campaigns, United States), DataBank, Lumen and Digital Realty. Source: twilio.com/en-us/legal/sub-processors · collected on Nov 10, 2026
