ActiveCampaign

United States · ActiveCampaign, LLC, Chicago, Illinois · Email marketing & newsletters

Official website ↗ Compare with…

ActiveCampaign is an email marketing and marketing automation platform for marketing teams, agencies and businesses that sell online. It is used to send campaigns and newsletters, build automated sequences and segment contacts, with a built-in CRM. SMS, WhatsApp and transactional email are available as add-ons.

Email campaigns and newsletters and multi-step marketing automation in every plan; transactional email (password resets, confirmations) as a separately sold add-on. Source: activecampaign.com/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Marketing features

Customer journey automation, segmentation (limited on Starter, premium on Enterprise), drag-and-drop email designer, A/B testing (email only on Starter and Plus, email and automations on Pro and Enterprise) and campaign reporting. Source: activecampaign.com/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Pricing

Free plan

The pricing page does not publish a free plan: it offers a 14-day free trial, no credit card required, and four paid plans. Source: activecampaign.com/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

First paid plan

Starter: billed yearly, for 1,000 contacts, with 10,000 email sends per month and one user seat; the captured page shows no amount. Source: activecampaign.com/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

What is billed

The number of contacts: the monthly price scales with list size, in bands, and included email sends are 10 to 15 times the contact limit depending on the plan. Source: activecampaign.com/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data & hosting

Company behind the service

ActiveCampaign, LLC, Chicago, Illinois (United States). Source: activecampaign.com/legal/terms-of-service · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data regions

Contact data can be hosted in the European Union for an account opened from Europe, the Middle East or Africa (based on the IP address at sign-up), since February or March 2024 depending on the country; the Conversations platform and beta features are excluded. Source: activecampaign.com/legal/mrdc-policy · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Declared cloud provider

Amazon Web Services for data storage, in the United States, the European Union and Australia (subprocessor list updated on September 25, 2026). Source: activecampaign.com/legal/subprocessors · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Subprocessor list

Public list updated on September 25, 2026: 7 subprocessors, including Amazon Web Services for storage and for AI, and 7 affiliates that may be involved depending on the customer’s location. Source: activecampaign.com/legal/subprocessors · collected on Nov 10, 2026

For developers

APIs and SDKs

A REST API that returns JSON, organized around resources (contacts, deals), webhooks, and App Studio to publish apps in the ActiveCampaign ecosystem. Source: developers.activecampaign.com · collected on Nov 10, 2026

AI tooling

An ActiveCampaign MCP server for Claude (URL under Settings > Developer) and an app for ChatGPT; Active Intelligence also connects to other apps’ MCP servers, in every plan. Source: activecampaign.com/platform/ai-mcp · collected on Nov 10, 2026
