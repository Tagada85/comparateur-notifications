Brevo

France · Brevo SAS, Paris · Email marketing & newsletters, Customer engagement, Transactional email

Official website ↗ Compare with…

Brevo is a French marketing and customer relationship platform for small and mid-sized businesses as well as larger companies. It sends campaigns and automated journeys by email, SMS, WhatsApp or push notifications, along with transactional messages via API or SMTP relay. It also bundles a CRM, live chat and a loyalty program.

Pricing

Free plan

Free plan: no time limit and no credit card; up to 300 emails per day once the account is approved for sending. Source: brevo.com/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

First paid plan

Starter: €7 per month billed monthly, or €76 per year billed annually, with 5,000 emails per month and up to 500 contacts. The grid also lists prices in US dollars ($9 per month). Source: brevo.com/pricing.md · collected on Nov 10, 2026

What is billed

The monthly email volume: each plan is bought by tier of emails per month, and each tier sets a maximum number of marketing contacts. Source: brevo.com/pricing.md · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data & hosting

Company behind the service

Brevo SAS, Paris (France), according to the general terms. Country-specific terms name another group company: Brevo Inc. for customers incorporated in the United States, Canada or Mexico, Brevo GmbH for Germany, Switzerland and Austria, and an Indian subsidiary for India. Source: brevo.com/legal/termsofuse · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data regions

Brevo says its servers are located in the European Union and that account data is encrypted before being backed up; the page does not tie this hosting to a plan. Source: brevo.com/features/data-security · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Declared cloud provider

OVH, with servers in France, and Google Cloud Platform, with servers in Belgium, for hosting; Cloudflare for content delivery and the web application firewall (annex 2 of the data processing agreement). Source: brevo.com/legal/termsofuse · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Subprocessor list

List published in annex 2 of the data processing agreement, part of the terms of service: 5 infrastructure subprocessors, 12 subprocessors tied to optional services (SMS, AI, integrations) and 5 Brevo group companies, each with its server location. Source: brevo.com/legal/termsofuse · collected on Nov 10, 2026

For developers

APIs and SDKs

A unified REST API to send email, SMS and WhatsApp messages and to manage contacts and events; official SDKs for Node.js, Python and PHP; SMTP relay and webhooks for real-time event tracking. Source: developers.brevo.com/docs/getting-started · collected on Nov 10, 2026

AI tooling

An MCP server to connect Brevo to AI assistants through the Model Context Protocol, and a guide to connect an agent to the API. Source: developers.brevo.com/docs/getting-started · collected on Nov 10, 2026
