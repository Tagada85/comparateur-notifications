MoEngage

United States · MoEngage, Inc., a Delaware corporation · Customer engagement

Official website ↗ Compare with…

MoEngage is a customer engagement platform for marketing and product teams running a mobile app or a website. It combines behavioral analytics, segmentation, automated journeys and messaging by push, email, SMS or in-app messages. Transactional messages go through a dedicated module, offered as an add-on.

Pricing

Free plan

The pricing page does not list a free plan: it presents two plans, Growth and Enterprise, with a one-year minimum commitment. Source: moengage.com/plans-and-pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

First paid plan

Growth, the first of the two plans: the pricing page publishes no price and points to the sales team; one-year minimum commitment. Source: moengage.com/plans-and-pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

What is billed

The monthly tracked user (MTU): each unique end-user profile with at least one session or one defined event in a calendar month, counted once; events generated by MoEngage, such as a notification received or an email sent, do not count. Source: moengage.com/plans-and-pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data & hosting

Company behind the service

MoEngage, Inc., a Delaware corporation (United States), which provides the website and services under the terms of use. Source: moengage.com/terms-of-use · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data regions

EU region available: the DC-02 data center, chosen at sign-up, alongside regions in the United States, India and Indonesia; once captured, data cannot be moved to another data center. Source: moengage.com/docs/api/introduction · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Declared cloud provider

Amazon Web Services (AWS), according to the pricing page FAQ, with separate data centers in the EU, North America and India. Source: moengage.com/plans-and-pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Subprocessor list

Public sub-processor list updated on September 1, 2026, published as a PDF document embedded in the page. Source: moengage.com/moengage-list-of-sub-processors · collected on Nov 10, 2026

For developers

APIs and SDKs

Native Android SDK (Kotlin, Java) and iOS SDK (Swift, Objective-C), a JavaScript web SDK that works with React, Angular or Vue, and cross-platform SDKs for React Native, Flutter, Cordova, Capacitor and Unity. Source: moengage.com/docs/developer-guide/introduction · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Workflows as code or via API

The REST APIs let you create and manage push and email campaigns without the dashboard; for Flows, they let you search them, read them and update their status. Source: moengage.com/docs/api/introduction · collected on Nov 10, 2026

AI tooling

An MCP server, included in both plans, to search campaigns, analyze performance metrics, review content and diagnose delivery issues from an AI assistant. Source: moengage.com/plans-and-pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026
