Mailgun

United States · Transactional email

Official website ↗ Compare with…

Mailgun is an email delivery service for developers who build email into their application, published by Sinch. Emails go out through an API or an SMTP relay, and inbound email can be parsed and passed on to the application. Additional tools validate addresses, track deliverability and preview messages.

Free plan

First paid plan

Basic: from $15 per month, 10,000 emails per month included, then from $1.80 per 1,000 emails. Source: mailgun.com/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

What is billed

The number of emails sent per month, beyond the volume included in the plan; the monthly plan cost and any extra emails or address validations are billed at the start of the following month. Source: mailgun.com/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Company behind the service

The entity shown on the service order, on behalf of Sinch Email (Sinch group); with Mailgun Technologies, Inc., the agreement is governed by Texas law and the courts of San Antonio (United States). Source: mailgun.com/legal/terms · collected on Nov 10, 2026

EU region offered at self-serve sign-up, like the US region; Australia requires a contract. Messages, event logs, statistics and suppression lists are processed and stored in the chosen region; account data stays global. Source: mailgun.com/about/regions · collected on Nov 10, 2026
