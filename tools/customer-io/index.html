Customer.io

United States · Peaberry Software, Inc., doing business as Customer.io, Beaverton, Oregon · Customer engagement

Official website ↗ Compare with…

Customer.io is a data-driven messaging platform for marketing, product and engineering teams. Messages are sent based on the events and attributes the application passes on: automations, broadcasts and transactional messages, by email, push, in-app, SMS or WhatsApp. An API, mobile SDKs and a CLI are used to integrate it.

Pricing

Free plan

The pricing page does not publish a free plan; a Startup Program offers up to 12 months free to early-stage companies that raised under $10 million. Source: customer.io/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

First paid plan

Essentials: from $100 per month, billed monthly, with 5,000 profiles and 1 million emails per month. Source: customer.io/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

What is billed

The profile (person or object): beyond the 5,000 profiles of Essentials, $0.009 per profile; beyond the included emails, $0.12 per 1,000 emails. Source: customer.io/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data & hosting

Company behind the service

Data regions

Region chosen when the account is created: United States or European Union (data center in Belgium); people data stays in the chosen region, for every workspace in the account. Source: docs.customer.io/accounts/settings/data-centers · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Declared cloud provider

The application is hosted on Google Cloud; the website and knowledge base on Amazon Web Services. Source: customer.io/legal/security · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Subprocessor list

Public list revised in October 2026: 9 entries, for infrastructure (including Amazon Web Services and Google Cloud), AI features, on by default and can be turned off, and communications; most in the United States and the EU. Source: customer.io/legal/sub-processors · collected on Nov 10, 2026

For developers

Pipelines, Track and App APIs, a Design Studio API, webhooks, and mobile SDKs for iOS, Android, React Native, Expo and Flutter. Source: docs.customer.io · collected on Nov 10, 2026
