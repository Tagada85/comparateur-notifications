Knock

United States · Knock Labs, Inc., New York · Notification infrastructure

Official website ↗ Compare with…

Knock is a notification infrastructure for product and engineering teams that send messages from their application. Workflows are set up in a dashboard or versioned with a CLI, then delivered in-app, by email or to team chat apps. SDKs provide a notification center and preferences to embed in the product.

Static or dynamic audiences (user segments), defined by rules on their properties; automated journeys (workflows) with delays, branches, batching and throttling; an experiment step that splits recipients into random cohorts for A/B testing; message templates in a drag-and-drop editor, with the Liquid language. What is billed

The message sent: one message delivered to one user on one channel. A trigger that produces an in-app message and an email counts as two. Source: knock.app/pricing · collected on Nov 5, 2026
