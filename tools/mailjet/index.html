Mailjet

France · Email marketing & newsletters, Transactional email

Official website ↗ Compare with…

Mailjet is a French email service, part of the Sinch group, for marketing teams and developers alike. Marketing teams build campaigns and newsletters in a visual editor, with segmentation and automations. Developers send transactional emails through an API or an SMTP relay.

Pricing

Free plan

Free plan: 6,000 emails per month, at most 200 per day, 1,000 contacts, no credit card required. Source: mailjet.com/fr/tarifs · collected on Nov 10, 2026

First paid plan

Starter: €8 per month excluding VAT, billed monthly, with 8,000 emails per month with no daily limit and 2,000 contacts; annual billing gives a 10% discount. Source: mailjet.com/fr/tarifs · collected on Nov 10, 2026

What is billed

The monthly email volume: each plan is chosen by tier of emails per month; beyond it, emails are billed per block of 1,000, and unused credits expire at the end of each billing cycle. Source: mailjet.com/fr/tarifs · collected on Nov 10, 2026
