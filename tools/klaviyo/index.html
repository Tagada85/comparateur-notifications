Klaviyo

United States · Klaviyo, Inc. · Customer engagement, Email marketing & newsletters

Official website ↗ Compare with…

Klaviyo is a customer marketing platform for brands that sell to consumers, especially in e-commerce. It brings together customer data, campaigns and automated flows across email, SMS, WhatsApp and push notifications, with integrations such as Shopify. Customer reviews, customer service and AI features round out the offering.

Pricing

Free plan

Free plan: up to 250 active profiles and 500 emails per month, with $5 of mobile messaging (SMS, MMS) per month. Source: klaviyo.com/fr/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

First paid plan

Email: $20 per month for 500 active profiles, with 5,000 emails per month; SMS is an add-on on this plan. Source: klaviyo.com/fr/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

What is billed

The active profile: any contact in the database who can be sent marketing messages; the pricing page sets the price by this number, from 500 to more than 150,000 profiles. Source: klaviyo.com/fr/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026
