Scaleway Transactional Email

France · Transactional email

Official website ↗ Compare with…

Scaleway Transactional Email (TEM) is the transactional email service of the European cloud provider Scaleway. It is meant for engineering teams that send messages users expect, such as account creation, orders or alerts, from an application, a website or a monitoring tool. Emails go out through an API or over SMTP; the service is reserved for transactional email and does not accept marketing sends.

Pricing

Free plan

Free plan: 300 emails per month; beyond that, only the excess emails are billed, at the end of the month. Source: scaleway.com/en/docs/transactional-email/faq · collected on Nov 10, 2026

First paid plan

Essential, with no subscription: €0.25 per 1,000 emails beyond the 300 included. First subscription plan, Scale: €80 per month, 100,000 emails included, then €0.20 per 1,000, with a dedicated IP and a 99.9% SLA; prices before tax. Source: scaleway.com/en/pricing/managed-services · collected on Nov 10, 2026

What is billed

The recipient: each recipient counts as one email; an email to one main recipient with three people in CC is billed as four emails. Source: scaleway.com/en/transactional-email-tem · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data & hosting

Company behind the service

Scaleway SAS, a simplified joint-stock company registered with the Paris trade register, headquartered in Paris (France). Source: scaleway.com/en/legal-notice · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data regions

Data hosted and processed entirely within the European Union: according to the FAQ, no TEM personal data is transferred outside the EU. Source: scaleway.com/en/docs/transactional-email/faq · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Declared cloud provider

Scaleway itself: according to the FAQ, the whole TEM technical stack is managed by Scaleway, within the EU, with no sub-processor outside the EU; in exceptional cases, across all its services, Scaleway may work with US or Canadian partners, under the safeguards of its DPA. Source: scaleway.com/en/docs/transactional-email/faq · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Subprocessor list

Public list shared by Scaleway’s services, reviewed in July 2025: seven colocation data centers, all processing in the EU (France, Italy, Netherlands, Poland, Sweden), three of them headquartered in the United States; the other listed subprocessors relate to other products, except hardware recycling, which covers all services. Source: scaleway.com/en/subprocessorlist · collected on Nov 10, 2026

For developers

APIs and SDKs

A REST API and an SMTP relay, Symfony and Zapier integrations, and webhooks (one per domain on Essential, unlimited on Scale). Source: scaleway.com/en/transactional-email-tem · collected on Nov 10, 2026
