Airship

United States · Airship Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation · Push & in-app messaging, Customer engagement

Official website ↗ Compare with…

Airship is a customer engagement platform built around mobile apps, for marketing and product teams running an app or a website. It orchestrates journeys across push, in-app messages, email, SMS, web and mobile wallet passes. No-code screens (Scenes) and mobile and web SDKs complement message sending.

The Pricing page shows no price: Airship Essentials is a self-service, no-code plan; the Airship Experience Platform (AXP) is quote-based. Source: airship.com/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

What is billed

Depending on the contract: monthly active users, monthly unique website visitors, contacts (counted once per channel) or notifications sent; email and SMS are counted in their own packages, Scenes per impression when the add-on is purchased. Source: airship.com/docs/reference/billing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data & hosting

Company behind the service

Airship Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation (United States), with its subsidiary Apptimize LLC. Source: airship.com/legal/subscription-terms · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data regions

Two cloud data centers, chosen by the customer: United States or European Union; the EU instance is hosted in Brussels (Belgium) and Frankfurt (Germany). Source: airship.com/legal/subprocessors · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Declared cloud provider

Google Cloud Platform for hosting and backup: in the United States for the US instance, in Brussels and Frankfurt for the EU instance (list posted on September 17, 2025, effective November 5, 2025). Source: airship.com/legal/subprocessors · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Subprocessor list

Public list posted on September 17, 2025, effective November 5, 2025: one table per data center (United States, EU) and per service (Airship, Apptimize), with each subprocessor’s processing location. Source: airship.com/legal/subprocessors · collected on Nov 10, 2026

For developers

APIs and SDKs

A REST API for messaging, audiences, event streaming and wallet; mobile SDKs and a web SDK for iOS, Android, the web, React Native, Flutter and other platforms. Source: airship.com/docs · collected on Nov 10, 2026
