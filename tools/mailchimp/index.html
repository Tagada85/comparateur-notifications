Mailchimp

United States · The Rocket Science Group LLC, doing business as Mailchimp, Atlanta, Georgia · Email marketing & newsletters, Transactional email

Official website ↗ Compare with…

Mailchimp is an email and SMS marketing platform from the Intuit group, aimed mainly at small businesses, online stores and non-profits. It is used to manage an audience, send campaigns and automate messages such as welcome or post-purchase follow-ups. A separate add-on, Mailchimp Transactional, sends emails triggered by an application.

Pricing

Free plan

Free plan: up to 250 contacts and 500 sends per month, with at most 250 sends per day, 1 audience and 1 seat. Source: mailchimp.com/help/about-mailchimp-pricing-plans · collected on Nov 10, 2026

First paid plan

Essentials: the starting price covers up to 500 contacts and rises with the contact count, up to 50,000; the monthly send limit is 10 times the contact limit. The help page does not state the amount and points to the pricing page. Source: mailchimp.com/help/about-mailchimp-pricing-plans · collected on Nov 10, 2026

What is billed

The number of contacts: the chosen tier sets the contact limit and the monthly send limit, and going over the contact limit is billed as an extra charge. The Pay As You Go plan sells email credits instead. Source: mailchimp.com/help/about-mailchimp-pricing-plans · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data & hosting

Company behind the service

The Rocket Science Group LLC, doing business as Mailchimp, Atlanta, Georgia (United States), part of the Intuit group. In the European Union, except Sweden and Denmark, payments are made to Intuit Ireland Software Limited. Source: mailchimp.com/fr/legal/terms · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data regions

The data processing addendum states that Mailchimp may transfer and process customer data in the United States and anywhere Mailchimp, its affiliates or its subprocessors carry out processing. Source: mailchimp.com/fr/legal/data-processing-addendum · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Declared cloud provider

The subprocessor list (updated on February 29, 2024) names Amazon, Washington State (United States), to facilitate delivery and temporary storage, and Akamai for application speed and security. Source: mailchimp.com/legal/subprocessors · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Subprocessor list

Public list updated on February 29, 2024: 25 subprocessors, 21 of them located in the United States; the others are in the United Kingdom, Germany, Romania, and for one of them the United States and Greece. Source: mailchimp.com/legal/subprocessors · collected on Nov 10, 2026

For developers

APIs and SDKs

Mailchimp Transactional (formerly Mandrill) sends transactional emails via API or SMTP and transactional SMS via API; webhooks report opens and clicks, and inbound emails can be received and processed. The developer portal also lists a separate Marketing API. Source: mailchimp.com/developer/transactional · collected on Nov 10, 2026
