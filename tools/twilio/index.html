Twilio

United States · Twilio Inc., a Delaware corporation, San Francisco · Messaging APIs: SMS, WhatsApp

Official website ↗ Compare with…

Twilio is a communication API platform for developers who add SMS, WhatsApp messages, voice calls, video or email to their applications. It is used for notifications, verification codes, campaigns and customer service conversations. Billing is usage-based, product by product.

Pricing

Free plan

A 30-day free trial, no credit card required, with free units per product, such as 100 SMS messages, 100 WhatsApp messages, 3,000 emails and 75 voice minutes; sending limited to verified recipients. Source: twilio.com/docs/usage/tutorials/how-to-use-your-free-trial-account · collected on Nov 10, 2026

First paid plan

Pay as you go: SMS from $0.0083 per message sent or received, WhatsApp from $0.005 per message, Twilio Email from $0.0013 per email; pricing current as of August 2026. Source: twilio.com/en-us/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

What is billed

Usage-based, product by product: the SMS, RCS or WhatsApp message sent or received, the email, the call minute, the verification; volume discounts as usage grows. Source: twilio.com/en-us/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data & hosting

Company behind the service

Twilio Inc., a Delaware corporation, San Francisco (United States); customers in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, among others, contract with Twilio Ireland Limited, Dublin (Ireland). Source: twilio.com/en-us/legal/tos · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data regions

For SMS sent through the Programmable Messaging API in the Ireland (IE1) region, messages are processed and stored exclusively in the EU up to the telecom carriers; support staff in non-EU countries may access the data. Source: twilio.com/docs/global-infrastructure/sms-eu-data-residency · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Declared cloud provider

AWS for core cloud hosting and data storage, in the United States, the EU and Australia (list updated in September 2026). Source: twilio.com/en-us/legal/sub-processors · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Subprocessor list

Public list updated in September 2026, by subprocessor, service and processing location; with Regional Twilio, for supported products, customer content is stored in the selected region (Ireland or Australia) and other account data is still processed in the United States. Source: twilio.com/en-us/legal/sub-processors · collected on Nov 10, 2026
