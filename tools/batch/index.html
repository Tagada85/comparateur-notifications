Batch

France · IMEDIAPP, a société anonyme (Batch), 43 rue Beaubourg, Paris · Customer engagement, Push & in-app messaging

Official website ↗ Compare with…

Batch is a French customer engagement platform designed for the CRM teams of brands running a mobile app and a website. It brings together customer data and the sending of mobile and web push, in-app messages, emails, SMS and WhatsApp messages, with automated journeys and AI agents. Mobile and web SDKs collect browsing and usage data.

Free plan

What is billed

The agreement bills the right to access the services, by thresholds set in the order form, not actual usage; SMS, WhatsApp and emails are billed on sending, not on receipt. Source: batch.com/service-agreement · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data & hosting

Company behind the service

IMEDIAPP, a société anonyme (Batch), 43 rue Beaubourg, Paris (France). Source: batch.com/service-agreement · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data regions

Customer data is stored exclusively in France, and Batch has no technical infrastructure outside the European Union. Source: doc.batch.com/guides-and-best-practices/privacy-and-gdpr/gdpr-compliance · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Declared cloud provider

The Platform page states dedicated physical servers (“bare metal”), 100% in Europe and not subject to the Cloud Act, without naming a hosting provider. Source: batch.com/platform · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Subprocessor list

The documentation’s GDPR page does not publish a subprocessor list; it states that Batch does not send personal data to external entities. Source: doc.batch.com/guides-and-best-practices/privacy-and-gdpr/gdpr-compliance · collected on Nov 10, 2026

For developers

APIs and SDKs

Server-side APIs (profiles, audiences, campaigns, exports, orchestrations, segments); mobile SDKs (iOS, Android, Flutter, React Native, Cordova) and a web SDK; webhooks among the Mobile Engagement Platform APIs. Source: doc.batch.com/developer · collected on Nov 10, 2026

AI tooling

The documentation explains how to expose the Batch API to an AI assistant through the MCP protocol, locally or server-side; the Batch team can help set it up, on request and for certain customer tiers.
