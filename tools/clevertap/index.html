CleverTap

United States · Customer engagement

Official website ↗ Compare with…

CleverTap is a customer engagement and retention platform for marketing and product teams, focused on mobile apps and websites. It connects user behavior analytics, segmentation and campaigns across several channels, from push to email, SMS and in-app messages. Mobile and web SDKs collect the events that feed these campaigns.

Mobile channels: mobile push, in-app messages, App Inbox and native display; web channels: web push, pop-ups and exit-intent messages; email delivered through the customer’s chosen email service provider, SMS, webhooks, and Facebook and Google Ads audiences; WhatsApp is also listed among campaign channels. Journeys and A/B tests of campaign messages in all three plans; lifecycle optimization (Lifecycle Optimizer) in the Ultimate plan; transactional messages with the Notify module, an add-on in every plan. Source: clevertap.com/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Marketing features
  • Contact segmentation

Behavioral and psychographic segmentation (RFM from the Pro plan), journeys, message A/B tests, an email editor, behavioral analytics (funnels, cohorts, trends) and custom dashboards, in all three plans. Source: clevertap.com/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Pricing

Free plan

The pricing page offers a free trial of the Core plan, with billing starting after 30 days; it does not present a permanent free plan. Source: clevertap.com/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

First paid plan

Core, for growing teams: a monthly price based on the selected tier of monthly active users, from “up to 5,000 MAU” to “100,000+ MAU”, excluding taxes and add-ons, billed after the 30-day trial. Source: clevertap.com/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

What is billed

Plan pricing is based on monthly active users (MAU) or data points, depending on the commercial model; AI features consume CleverAI tokens, with an allowance included in each plan. Source: clevertap.com/pricing · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data & hosting

Company behind the service

Depending on the customer’s location, the contracting entity may be WizRocket Inc., a Delaware corporation based in San Francisco (United States), CleverTap Private Limited (India) or CleverTap Pte. Ltd. (Singapore). Source: clevertap.com/privacy-policy · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Data regions

Data storage and processing in Europe (EU region, the default), India, the United States, Singapore, Indonesia and the United Arab Emirates; the region is enabled by contacting CleverTap. Source: developer.clevertap.com/docs/idc · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Declared cloud provider

Amazon Web Services, named as its hosting provider in the security documentation. Source: docs.clevertap.com/docs/security-overview · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Subprocessor list

The privacy policy points to a current list of sub-processors, available through CleverTap’s Trust Portal. Source: clevertap.com/privacy-policy · collected on Nov 10, 2026

For developers

APIs and SDKs

Mobile SDKs for Android, iOS, React Native, Flutter, Cordova, Unity and KaiOS, a JavaScript web SDK, and Node.js and Python server-side libraries to call the API. Source: developer.clevertap.com/docs/clevertap-sdks · collected on Nov 10, 2026

Workflows as code or via API

The REST API can create campaigns from events in external systems, for example a price-drop alert sent as a push notification; webhooks trigger backend workflows on tracked events. Source: developer.clevertap.com/docs/api-overview · collected on Nov 10, 2026
