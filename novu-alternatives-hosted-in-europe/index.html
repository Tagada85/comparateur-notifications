Novu alternatives hosted in Europe

Where Novu stands: EU region in Frankfurt (Germany), chosen when the account is created; other regions (United Kingdom, Singapore, Australia, Japan, South Korea) on Enterprise plans. (source, November 5, 2026) Data residency in the United States or the EU is included from the free plan up. (source, November 5, 2026)

When the region is a given, looking for an alternative hosted in Europe means comparing the rest: the hosting provider, the subprocessors, the country of the company behind the service and the deployment model. This page goes over these points based on the vendors’ pages collected from November 4 to 5, 2026. Vendors are presented in alphabetical order; the list is not exhaustive, and none pays to be on it.

What Novu publishes

Novu

Declared cloud provider
The Security page of the documentation gives the regions, not the cloud provider. (source, November 5, 2026)
Subprocessor list
Novu’s trust center only renders with JavaScript: no subprocessor list can be read in the served page. (source, November 5, 2026)
Company behind the service
Noti-Fire Apps Ltd., Ramat Gan (Israel), with a representative in the EU under Article 27 of the GDPR. (source, November 5, 2026)
Deployment model
A managed service (Novu Cloud) or a self-hosted community edition, which lacks some Novu Cloud features. (source, November 4, 2026) The code is published under the MIT license, except the “enterprise” code, which is under a commercial license. (source, November 4, 2026)

The other vendors that offer to keep data in the European Union

Courier

Data regions
Two separate datacenters, in the United States and in the EU (Ireland), with no shared data; the EU datacenter is available on paid plans (Business and Enterprise). (source, November 5, 2026)
Declared cloud provider
Amazon Web Services for cloud infrastructure, in the United States and in the EU (subprocessor list updated on August 3, 2026). (source, November 5, 2026)
Subprocessor list
Public list updated on August 3, 2026: 15 subprocessors, 9 of them located in “USA & EU” and 6 in the United States only. (source, November 5, 2026)
Company behind the service
trycourier.com, Inc., San Francisco (United States). (source, November 5, 2026)

SuprSend

Data regions
The Security page mentions data residency options in the EU, without naming a region. (source, November 5, 2026)
Declared cloud provider
The Security page does not name a cloud provider. (source, November 5, 2026)
Subprocessor list
The Security page has a “Data Subprocessors” section, but no list in the served text. (source, November 5, 2026)
Company behind the service
SuprStack Inc.; its terms of service are governed by the laws of Delaware (United States). (source, November 5, 2026)

Not yet, or not stated

The two other vendors compared do not publish a European Union region available today:

Knock
Knock says it expects to offer data residency in the European Union by the end of 2026. (source, November 5, 2026)
MagicBell
The Security page claims GDPR compliance, without stating a hosting region. (source, November 5, 2026)

Before switching tools

  • If jurisdiction is what matters, the company behind the service weighs as much as the region: read its row for each vendor.
  • Each vendor bills a different unit: how much does notification infrastructure cost?
  • Novu against another vendor, criterion by criterion: Knock or Novu? and Novu or SuprSend?

What this page does not cover

This page is not legal advice. It does not compare certifications, the content of data processing agreements or transfer mechanisms; see also hosting, EU region and subprocessors. It relies on the vendors’ public pages. Has a piece of information changed? Report it.
